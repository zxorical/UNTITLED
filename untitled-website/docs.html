Getting Started

Untitled is built to make VRFS giveaways easier to discover, track and join.

Create an account, connect your Discord account and configure the servers you want Untitled to monitor.

Giveaway Tracker

Untitled monitors supported giveaway channels and automatically keeps track of new giveaways.

Tracked giveaways can be viewed through your Untitled account and notifications can be configured to keep you updated.

AutoJoiner

AutoJoiner is a Premium feature that automatically joins all giveaways for you.

You remain in control of which features are enabled through your account settings.
